## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate at American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB)**, graduating in 2026.

My work sits at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Data Science, Full-Stack Development, and Banking Technology**.

I'm a **published co-author in the Journal of Imaging (MDPI)**, where my research focuses on bilingual sign language recognition using **YOLOv11**.

I enjoy building practical systems that combine software engineering with intelligent technologies — from computer vision models and data science workflows to banking applications and enterprise database systems.
